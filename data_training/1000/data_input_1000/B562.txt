KATE HOLLENBECK								04-08-03


Patient History

Kate’s problems apparently began when she was a young teenager. She fortunately has not had any significant pain problems with her condition but has undergone significant condylar resorption and retrusion of the mandible. She occludes now only on her second molars.

Kate has been under orthodontic treatment for some time, and apparently her occlusion is fairly well aligned to proceed on with surgery. 

Basic Diagnoses

Based on available records, which include the MRI, photographs, cephalometric tracing, as well as reports from other physicians, her diagnoses are as follows:

    1. Bilateral TMJ idiopathic condylar resorption
    2. Posterior vertical maxillary deficiency
    3. AP mandibular deficiency
    4. Transverse maxillary deficiency
    5. Anterior open bite
    6. Occluding only on the second molars
    7. Headaches 
    8. Myofascial pain
    9. Decreased oropharyngeal airway
    10. Class II occlusion
    11. High occlusal plane angulation

Recommended Treatment

    1. Presurgical orthodontics
    A. Continue to align and level maxillary arch
    B. Continue to align and level mandibular arch

    2. Surgery
    A. Bilateral TMJ condylectomies, meniscectomies, debridement, and reconstruction with TMJ Concepts total joint prostheses
    B. Multiple maxillary osteotomies to expand the maxilla, downgraft the posterior aspect, and level the occlusal plane
    C. Bilateral mandibular coronoidectomies
    D. Bilateral TMJ fat grafts (harvested from abdomen)

    3. Postsurgical orthodontics to refine occlusion

This plan should get her the best possible functional and aesthetic result.

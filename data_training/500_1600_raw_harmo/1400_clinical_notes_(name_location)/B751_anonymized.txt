I believe this treatment plan can get <PERSON> the best possible functional and esthetic result.  It would be best to delay her surgery at least for a few years until she is closer to the age of 13 or 14.  The reason for this is because of the subsequent effects of maxillary growth and development and the alteration of facial growth factor with this type of surgery.  However, if she does develop significant sleep apnea problems then it may be an advantage to proceed earlier on with the treatment.

I suggested taking bicuspids in all four quadrants.  The upper incisors can actually be retracted and be in a more vertical inclination because the incisor angulation will increase about 10 degrees with the counterclockwise rotation of the maxilla at the time of surgery.  Likewise, the lower incisors can be significantly over-angulated since the lower incisor angulation will decrease approximately 17 degrees with the surgery.
